# ADRIFT

A first-person space exploration sim about how little you matter.
One self-contained HTML file, no dependencies, built for iOS Safari.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser (or serve the repo and
visit it on your phone). Works best fullscreen on a phone held in one hand
in a dark room.

- right of screen — look · left of screen — drift stick
- hold THRUST to burn, HALT to stop
- desktop: mouse look, WASD, SHIFT to burn, X to stop

Contents: a sun, six worlds, four moons, a ringed giant, city lights on a
night side no one is looking at, a black hole with gravitationally lensed
accretion disk, and the occasional supernova that nobody mourns.

Speed scales with emptiness: you crawl near worlds and streak across the
void at multiples of c, and it still takes too long. That is the point.

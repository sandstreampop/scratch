# Definition of Done — "Romantiskt landskap" explorable

Source: photo of Elias Martin, *Romantiskt landskap* (before 1777, oil on canvas).

1. **Camera scope: B — fly inside.** Free flight past the foreground rock and out over the water toward the ship, inside a soft bounded volume so the layered illusion never breaks.
2. **Liveliness: B — alive but true.** Water ripples and glints, clouds drift, light shafts through the tree, flag and sails stir, a bird crosses, ambient water and wind. Same painting, breathing.
3. **Delivery: C — staged.** v1 pushed fast so it can be tried on a phone, then a hardened v2 with screenshot verification from many camera poses, performance tuning, and iteration until told to stop.
4. **Mobile first.** iOS Safari touch controls (right thumb looks, left thumb drifts, auto-glide when idle). Desktop mouse + WASD second.
5. **Context in the experience.** Title card and info panel with the museum wall text (sv/en).
